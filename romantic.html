ROMANTIC PLACES TO DATE IN CEBU CITY.

Top of Cebu Restaurant

A premier open-air restaurant perched along the mountain ridges, serving Filipino and fusion favorites with sweeping panoramas of the Metro Cebu skyline. Cebu Tops Road, Busay, Cebu City. Tucked right inside the famous "Tops" observation plaza complex.

La Vie in the Sky

A French-inspired wine-and-dine spot featuring whimsical European decor, fairy lights, and expansive nighttime overviews of the city. Cebu Tops Road, Malubog, Cebu City. Situated just a few minutes further up the road past Top of Cebu.

Mr. A Bar & Restaurant

A classic hillside staple known for relaxed sunset drinks and warm, romantic dinners under the stars. Nivel Hills, Lower Busay Heights, Budlaan - Busay Road, Cebu City. Located on the lower portion of the mountain ridge, making it a quicker uphill trip than Tops.

Temple of Leah

A grand, Greek-inspired Roman architectural shrine offering majestic pillars, sweeping staircases, and romantic dusk lighting over the valley. Cebu Transcentral Highway, Busay, Cebu City. Positioned on a prominent hillside terrace visible along the main mountain highway.

La Vie Parisienne

A Parisian-themed bakery, deli, and wine cellar surrounded by glowing pink trees and a cozy European ambiance. 371 Gorordo Avenue, Lahug, Cebu City. Conveniently located along the main Gorordo Avenue strip, set inside the iconic historic "Pink House" estate.

Circa 1900

Set inside a restored 1900s Spanish-colonial home, this venue serves homestyle Filipino-European classics in a lush, heritage garden setting. Sanjercasvil Extension Road, Lahug, Cebu City. Hidden inside a peaceful, gated residential lane right off the busier city streets.

Highlights Lounge at Harolds Hotel

A chic rooftop lounge where couples can sip cocktails and enjoy music against the glittering backdrop of the city lights. Roof Deck, Harolds Hotel, Gorordo Avenue corner Rosal Street, Lahug, Cebu City. Located on the very top floor of the hotel, right at the intersection of Gorordo Avenue.
